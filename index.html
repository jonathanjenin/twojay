How we work

From first idea to first revenue.

We step in where the company is. Some founders need help proving the problem. Others need an MVP shipped or a round closed.

01

Validate

Pressure-test the idea before money and months go into it.

  • User interviews and market validation
  • Pitch and value proposition
  • Legal and compliance constraints
  • Path to a first paying customer
02

Build

Lead product and engineering until something real ships.

  • User journey and feature architecture
  • Proof of concept, then market-ready MVP
  • First hires and team processes
03

Grow

Find repeatable revenue and keep the company funded.

  • Go-to-market strategy and first growth
  • OKR-driven product roadmap
  • Fundraising alongside lead investors
  • SR&ED, IRAP and government programs
